Website Analytics

With your consent, we use PostHog to collect anonymous usage data to help us improve this website and understand our audience. Analytics are only activated after you explicitly accept via the consent banner.

What we collect

  • Page views (URL visited, referrer, browser type, operating system, screen size)
  • Page leave events
  • Interaction events (e.g. which app card was clicked)

We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or any other personally identifiable information. No cookies or persistent identifiers are stored on your device (tracking uses in-memory storage only).

Purpose

To understand how visitors use this website so we can improve its content and user experience.

Legal basis

Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), provided via the analytics consent banner displayed on this website.

Data processor and hosting

Analytics data is processed by PostHog B.V. on their EU-hosted infrastructure (eu.i.posthog.com). No personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA. PostHog acts as a data processor. You can review their privacy practices and Data Processing Agreement at posthog.com/privacy.

Retention

Anonymous analytics data is retained in accordance with PostHog's standard data retention policies. Because the data is anonymous and contains no personal identifiers, it cannot be linked back to individual visitors.

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the page footer. This will stop all future analytics tracking. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to restriction — request that we limit processing of your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to the processing of your personal data
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw your analytics consent at any time via "Cookie Settings"

Because our analytics data is anonymous and cannot identify individuals, there is generally no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete. However, you may contact us with any data privacy concerns at vibeapps.contact@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO): uodo.gov.pl.

Data Storage

All app state and preferences are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Our apps do not use external databases, cloud storage, or remote servers.

Some apps may use your personal iCloud account to sync data across your devices. This data is managed by Apple and is accessible only to you. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data exposure from external systems.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
