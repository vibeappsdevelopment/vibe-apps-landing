Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 6, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Vibe Apps ("we", "our", or "us") handles information across our website and applications.

Your privacy is important. Each of our apps has its own specific privacy policy detailing how it handles your data:

  • 365 Vibe Days Privacy Policy
  • Vibe Eyes Privacy Policy

In general, our apps do not collect, store, process, or share any personal data. They work fully offline and do not require accounts, registration, or network access.

Information We Collect

None.

Our apps do not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Usage data
  • Identifiers
  • Location data
  • Analytics
  • Crash reports

No data is transmitted to any servers.

Analytics & Privacy Standards

We use PostHog to collect anonymous usage data to help us improve this website and understand our audience. To protect your privacy and comply with GDPR and CCPA standards, this tracking is strictly "cookieless." We do not store tracking cookies on your device or track your activity across other websites.

This data does not include personally identifiable information. You can learn more about PostHog's privacy practices at posthog.com/privacy.

Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data. Because our apps and website do not collect or store any identifiable personal profiles, there is generally no data for us to delete or provide. However, you may contact us with any data privacy concerns.

Third-Party Services

Our apps do not use third-party services that collect data.

Data Storage

All app state and preferences are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Our apps do not use external databases, cloud storage, or remote servers.

Some apps may use your personal iCloud account to sync data across your devices. This data is managed by Apple and is accessible only to you. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data exposure from external systems.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
