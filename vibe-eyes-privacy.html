Privacy Policy for Vibe Eyes

Last Updated: February 28, 2026

At Vibe Apps, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how the Vibe Eyes macOS application ("the App") handles your information.

Our core principle is simple: Vibe Eyes does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Vibe Eyes is designed to run entirely locally on your Mac. We do not collect any personal information, analytics, usage data, or crash reports directly. The App does not require an account, and we do not track your behavior.

2. Accessibility Permissions & Cursor Tracking

To function correctly, Vibe Eyes requires macOS Accessibility permissions. This permission is used strictly and exclusively to monitor your cursor's global position on the screen so that the animated eyes can follow your cursor across multiple monitors.

  • No Data Logging: We do not log, record, or store your cursor movements, clicks, or any other input.
  • No Data Transmission: Cursor coordinates and movement data never leave your device and are never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.
  • Real-time Processing: The cursor tracking data is processed in real-time in the device’s volatile memory solely for the purpose of rendering the app's animations.

3. App Settings and Preferences

Your user preferences (such as selected characters, themes, and customized colors) are saved locally on your device using Apple's standard @AppStorage mechanisms. We do not sync or have access to these settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Vibe Eyes does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tracking, or advertising frameworks. The app operates entirely standalone on your device.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If any significant changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
