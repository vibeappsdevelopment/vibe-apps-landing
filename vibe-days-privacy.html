Privacy Policy for 365 Vibe Days

Last Updated: March 6, 2026

At Vibe Apps, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how the 365 Vibe Days iOS application ("the App") handles your information.

Our core principle is simple: 365 Vibe Days does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

365 Vibe Days is designed to run entirely locally on your device. We do not collect any personal information, analytics, usage data, or crash reports directly. The App does not require an account, and we do not track your behavior.

2. App Settings and Preferences

Your user preferences and data are saved locally on your device or synced securely via your personal Apple iCloud account. We do not have access to this data, nor is it stored on any of our own servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Website Analytics

The 365 Vibe Days application does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tracking, or advertising frameworks. The app operates entirely standalone on your device.

Please note that this website uses PostHog to collect anonymous usage data. To protect your privacy and comply with GDPR and CCPA standards, this tracking is strictly "cookieless." We do not store tracking cookies on your device or track your activity across other websites.

Because we do not collect or store identifiable personal profiles, there is generally no data for us to delete or provide under GDPR/CCPA. You can learn more about PostHog's privacy practices at posthog.com/privacy.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If any significant changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
