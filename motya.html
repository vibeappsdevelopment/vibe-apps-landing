What it does

  • 01

    In store order

    Products are grouped by department, in the order you walk the aisles, so you go through the store once and don't double back.

  • 02

    Type it your way

    “eggs 10”, “2 kg potatoes”, “milk x2” — Motya gets the amount. Not in the catalog? Press Return and it's on the list anyway.

  • 03

    Prices

    In Belarus with the app in Russian, about 650 products come with typical store prices. Anywhere else, 181 everyday products in your language: type the shelf price once and Motya remembers it.

  • 04

    Widget

    Your list on the Home Screen. Tick items off without opening the app; they're ticked in the app too.

  • 05

    Lists for everything

    Groceries, the cottage, the cat, a wish list. Finish a list and carry over what you didn't buy, or repeat an old one in one tap.

  • 06

    Spending

    What the month cost, by department and by week, from the prices you actually paid.

Support

Questions, a bug, a product missing from the catalog — write to vibeapps.contact@gmail.com, in English or Russian.

Where are my lists stored?

Only on your iPhone. Motya has no account and no server, so nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes the lists.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and pick Motya. It shows the open list; a tap on a line ticks it.

Why are there no prices?

Store prices come with the Belarusian catalog: country Belarus, app in Russian. In other countries and languages, type the price from the shelf once — Motya keeps it for next time.

How do I finish a list?

Open the list and tap ··· → Finish list, or swipe a list to the left on My lists. If something wasn't bought, Motya offers to carry it over to a new list.

How do I start over?

Settings → Clear history deletes finished lists, spending and learned prices. Open lists stay.
