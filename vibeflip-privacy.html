Privacy Policy for Vibe Flip

Last Updated: June 27, 2026

At Vibe Apps, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how the Vibe Flip iOS application and its Home Screen widget ("the App") handle your information.

Our core principle is simple: Vibe Flip does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Vibe Flip is designed to run entirely locally on your device. We do not collect any personal information, analytics, usage data, or crash reports directly. The App does not require an account, and we do not track your behavior.

2. Your Quotes, Moods & Journal Entries

Vibe Flip lets you record daily reflections — such as your mood, energy, and focus — and builds your journal, analytics, and calendar views from them. All of this content is created and kept by you, for you.

  • Stored On Your Device: Your reflections and journal entries are saved locally on your device.
  • Optional iCloud Sync: If iCloud is enabled, your data may sync securely across your own devices through your personal Apple iCloud account. We do not have access to this data, nor is it stored on any of our own servers.
  • No Transmission To Us: Your entries never leave your device and Apple's iCloud; they are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. App Settings and Preferences

Your user preferences (such as selected quote categories, themes, and notification settings) are saved locally on your device or synced securely via your personal Apple iCloud account. We do not have access to this data, nor is it stored on any of our own servers.

4. Third-Party Services & Website Analytics

The Vibe Flip application does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tracking, or advertising frameworks. The app operates entirely standalone on your device.

This website uses PostHog (EU-hosted) to collect anonymous usage data, only after you provide explicit consent via the analytics banner. No cookies or persistent identifiers are stored. No personally identifiable information is collected.
